# Maintainers

This is a Typelevel organizational project.

## Active Maintainers

The maintainers listed here have committed to looking after this repository.
Once a year, they are asked by Typelevel whether they are still committed. The
**Last Confirmed** column shows the date of their most recent "yes".

What a recent confirmation means:

- A person who knows the project has confirmed that it is not abandoned.
- Someone is there to handle security reports, pull requests and issues.

What it does **not** mean:

- It is not a promise of response times, new features or regular releases.
- It does not rate the project's quality or activity. A quiet project with few
  commits or releases can be stable and finished rather than abandoned.

If nobody answers the yearly request, even after reminders, the project is
handed over to the Typelevel process for projects without active maintainers.
